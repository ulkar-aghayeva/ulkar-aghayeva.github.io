  <div><p>My playlists contain music that I felt compelled to save outside of their original albums. I use Apple Music, so all my playlists live there and will be linked in this post. They are usually organized by composer, theme or a particular piece where I collect different recordings or instrumental arrangements of the same piece. I’ll list them in reverse chronological order (2024-2018).</p><p><a href="https://music.apple.com/us/playlist/machaut/pl.u-xlyNNaYCkRgRxvj" rel="noopener"><strong>Guillaume de Machaut</strong></a>: 3 lovely chansons by a 14th century French composer</p><p><a href="https://music.apple.com/us/playlist/agnus-dei-b-minor-mass/pl.u-KVXBBa1IZMAM407" rel="noopener"><strong>Agnus Dei from B minor mass BWV 232</strong></a>: a collection of recordings by various choirs</p><p><a href="https://music.apple.com/us/playlist/faur%C3%A9-2nd-3rd-movements/pl.u-xlyNNxPCkRgRxvj" rel="noopener"><strong>Fauré 2nd/3rd movements</strong></a>: 2nd movements of his two cello sonatas &amp; 3rd movements of his two piano quartets (that is, the best movements)</p><p><a href="https://music.apple.com/us/playlist/quando-corpus-morietur/pl.u-kv9llrdF7qAqzDx" rel="noopener"><strong>Quando corpus morietur</strong></a>: last duo from Pergolesi’s Stabat Mater; two renditions</p><p><a href="https://music.apple.com/us/playlist/cello-sarabandes/pl.u-2aoqqAyuNBDBR2E" rel="noopener"><strong>Cello Sarabandes</strong></a>: a cross-section of Bach cello suites at their 4th movements, the Sarabandes.
